# GLOSSARY: coffee subscription pause

| Term | Meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Box | One bag of coffee sent to a subscriber every 28 days. | shipment, parcel |
| Subscriber | The person who pays for the subscription and manages it on the account page. | customer, user, member |
| Skip | Move the next box (and its charge) forward by one 28-day cycle. | defer, postpone |
| Pause | One or more skips in a row, at most 3 boxes, chosen by the subscriber. | hold, freeze, suspend |
| Unskip | Undo the most recent skip: the next box moves back 28 days, if that date is still 3+ days away. | unpause, resume early |
| Charge | The card payment taken 2 days before a box ships. | bill run |
| Gift | A prepaid subscription for a set number of boxes, bought by someone else. | present sub |
| Gift recipient | The person who receives a gift's boxes; not a subscriber, cannot change anything. | giftee |
